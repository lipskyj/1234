What We're Actually Measuring

Completion signal.
Not vanity metrics.



Walk Comments
Comments saying "went for a walk," "just got back," "touched grass." These are the primary KPI — a real action taken.
PRIMARY SIGNAL
Saves & Sends
Saved to show someone else = the message landed. Sent to a contact = personal accountability loop activated.
DISTRIBUTION SIGNAL
Reels Completion
Completion rate on Reels — not likes. If they watched to the end, the pattern interrupt worked mid-scroll.
ATTENTION SIGNAL
Stitch / Duet Rate
People adding their "went for a walk" video on top = organic amplification that stays on-brand automatically.
AMPLIFICATION
Poll Responses
Both poll options work — but the ratio of "today" to "can't remember" tells us what the audience actually needs.
AUDIENCE SIGNAL
Streak Participants
People who log Day 2, Day 3 in comments. Returning to report = the campaign broke the scroll loop at least once.
RETENTION SIGNAL

Brand Constitution — Never Break These

The account always sounds
like it's mocking the feed
from inside it.



01
Never preach.
No "social media is bad for you" lectures. The tone is blunt irony — the feed already knows. So do we.
02
The joke lands first.
Hook with the bait pattern. Land the punchline. The action is the punchline. In that order, every time.
03
No guilt without exit.
Every guilt-tactic post ends with one concrete, free, frictionless action. Walk. Water. Close the app.
04
Bilingual, not translated.
Spanish captions are rewritten for the register, not Google Translated. They should feel native to each language.
05
Character stays intact.
No inspirational quotes. No wellness language. No "you deserve rest." We live in the feed. We mock the feed.
06
Never explain the bit.
If you have to caption what the tactic is doing, the tactic failed. Let the format do the work.
SCROLLSTOP
Using the feed's own attention mechanics against itself. Every bait tactic flipped to push people off their phone and into the world. Never breaking character.
